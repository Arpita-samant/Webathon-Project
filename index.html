FAQS

1. What is Cognizance? +

Cognizance is the annual technical festival of IIT Roorkee, showcasing innovation, technical events, workshops, and guest lectures from industry leaders.

2. What is a Pronite? +

Pronites are the flagship pro-nights held during Cognizance, featuring live music concerts, comedy shows, and celebrity performances.

3. How to register for Cognizance 2026? +

You can register by clicking the Login/Register button on top right, creating an account, and choosing your desired event or pass package.

4. What is included in the CLASSIC Pass? +

The CLASSIC Pass grants you entry to all main technical competitions, guest lectures, exhibitions, and general pronite areas.

5. How to register for Events in Cognizance 2026? +

Navigate to the 'Events' section in the navbar, select your preferred domain, and click 'Register' on the individual event page.

FAQS

1. What is Nirvan Tech Fest? +

Nirvan Tech Fest is a technology-focused fest that brings together students, innovators, and tech enthusiasts through exciting competitions, workshops, and interactive activities.

2. Who can participate in Nirvan Tech Fest? +

Students and technology enthusiasts who meet the eligibility requirements of the respective events can participate in Nirvan Tech Fest.

3. How can I register for Nirvan Tech Fest? +

Click on the Register button on the website, fill in the required details, and submit the registration form to participate in the fest.

4. What events are available at Nirvan Tech Fest? +

Nirvan Tech Fest features technical competitions, workshops, challenges, and engaging activities designed to encourage creativity, innovation, and technical skills.

5. Can I participate in multiple events? +

Yes, participants can participate in multiple events, provided they meet the requirements and the event timings do not overlap.
